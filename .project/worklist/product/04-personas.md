## 1. Purpose

Describe representative user types, their goals and frustrations, and how they may use Worklist. These personas are hypotheses until validated with research.

## 2. Primary Persona — Team Lead

**Name:** Ahmed  
**Age:** 28 (illustrative)  
**Role:** Team lead at a six-person startup  
**Tech level:** Medium

**Goals:**

- See team tasks in one place.
- Find overdue work.
- Assign work quickly.

**Frustrations:**

- Shared sheets become difficult to manage.
- Some tools feel too expensive or complex.
- Changes may not be visible to the team quickly enough.

**Possible usage:** Opens the list daily, sorts by deadline, filters tasks assigned to himself, and shares a view with teammates.

## 3. Secondary Persona — Freelancer

**Name:** Sana  
**Age:** 24 (illustrative)  
**Role:** Freelance designer  
**Tech level:** Low

**Goals:**

- Track client projects.
- Avoid missed deadlines.
- Use a simple tool without much training.

**Frustrations:**

- Complex tools can be overwhelming.
- Notes may not provide enough structure.

**Possible usage:** Creates items per client, uses a due-date column, and filters by client.

## 4. Tertiary Persona — Power User

**Name:** Bilal  
**Age:** 32 (illustrative)  
**Role:** Operations manager  
**Tech level:** High

**Goals:**

- Edit many items efficiently.
- Reuse saved views.
- Integrate work through an API if one is provided.

**Frustrations:**

- Bulk operations may be limited.
- Keyboard workflows may be missing.

**Possible usage:** Uses bulk selection and saved views; API access is not yet committed for v1.

## 5. Anti-Persona — Enterprise Administrator (Initial Exclusion)

An enterprise administrator may require SSO, audit logs, compliance features, and fine-grained roles. These needs are outside the current v1 hypothesis and require separate validation before inclusion.

## 6. Example Scenario

Ahmed opens Worklist on Monday, filters open work, sorts by due date, and assigns several overdue tasks. The two-minute completion target from the original example is aspirational and should be measured with users.
